MANDATORY PROTOCOLS • 5-Pax Group & Convoy Safeguards:
• Strict Dry Day (Oct 2): Gandhi Jayanti is a 100% legal dry day across Karnataka. Purchase all homestay alcohol in Bangalore on Thursday, Oct 1st and transport sealed in car boot.
• Mullayanagiri Vehicle Entry Pass: Pre-book Slot 1 (6:00 AM – 12:00 PM) on the official portal (pgbiz.omniware.in/chikkamagalurutourism). Download or screenshot QR pass prior to ascent (cellular data drops at Kaimara checkpost).
• Solo Driver & Rider Relief: Exactly 1 car driver and 1 biker. Highway speed is capped at 75–85 km/h. If driver/rider fatigue hits post-Jhari Falls on Day 2, toggle to Track B (Hidden Gems) to skip 106 km of driving.
• Boot Space Rule: 5 adults in a hatchback requires soft duffel bags or backpacks only. Strictly zero hard-shell suitcases.
• Emergency Cash Pool: Designate one treasurer to hold ₹5,000 in cash for Jhari 4x4 Jeeps, parking, and rural stalls where UPI fails.

Day 1: Friday, Oct 2 – Bangalore Exit, Mydala Kayaking & Sunset Heritage

Time Route Segment / Stop Details, Activities & Equal Group Allocations (5 Pax)[cite: 1]
06:30 AM – 08:45 AM Whitefield → Goraguntepalya → Mydala Lake (88 km) Pacing: 06:30 AM rollout accounts for group morning preparation. Convoy cruises along NH 48 at 75–85 km/h. Car matches Meteor 350 speed.
Pre-Breakfast Munchies: Eat Bangalore-packed light bites (energy bars, bananas, dry snacks) in car to maintain stamina.
08:45 AM – 10:30 AM Mydala Lake & Mandaragiri Base Activities (Zero Temple Climbing):
• Earthen lake bund stroll & granite boulder exploration.
• Water Sports Group Allocation: 2 Tandem Kayaks (2 + 2 = 4 pax) + 1 Solo Kayak/SUP (1 pax) (~₹250–₹300/head) operated by local adventure guides.
• 10-min stroll to Mandaragiri base to photograph the iconic peacock-feather dome architecture (zero steps/rituals).
• Open-air lakeside coffee from personal flask.
10:30 AM – 10:50 AM Mydala Lake → Kyathsandra (12 km) Short 20-min rural drive to Karnataka's Thatte Idli culinary strip.
10:50 AM – 12:00 PM Hotel Pavithra, Kyathsandra (Brunch) Brunch Stop: Steaming hot Thatte Idlis served with dollops of white butter, spicy red coconut chutney, crispy vadas, and potato saagu.
Driver Rest: Full 1-hour physical halt for solo driver and biker to recharge before the long highway run.
12:00 PM – 04:00 PM Kyathsandra → Tiptur → Kadur → Chikmagalur (170 km) Route: Smooth 2-lane NH 73. Straightforward driving with bypasses around major towns.
Mandatory Fatigue Buffer: 25-min stop near Arsikere bypass for tender coconut, washroom break, and limb stretching.
04:00 PM – 05:15 PM Homestay Check-in & Freshen Up Check-in at booked plantation stay in Kaimara/Mallandur foothills (Exact stay TBD). Unpack, wash off road grime, and rest for 1 hour.
05:15 PM – 06:45 PM Evening Sunset Options:
Opt A: Mallandur Shooting Point
Opt B: MG Park / Rathnagiri Bore 		Option A (Recommended Scenic Ridge): Drive 15 mins to Mallandur Shooting Point. Open grassland cliff edge dropping into coffee valleys with 360° sunset views (quiet, zero crowds).
Option B (Town Park): Visit Mahatma Gandhi Park for an elevated northern town view.
07:00 PM – 08:30 PM Panduranga Coffee (Operating Since 1938) Visit the heritage institution on MG Road. Group coffee tasting; shop for fresh roasted Arabica powder, chicory blends, estate black pepper, and artisanal cocoa chocolates.
08:30 PM onwards Dinner & Wind Down Dinner at The Arabica or homestyle dinner at the stay. Sleep early for sunrise alarm.

Day 2: Saturday, Oct 3 – Mullayanagiri Peak, Jhari Waterfall Dip & Afternoon Circuit

Driver Fatigue Management • Afternoon Plan Selector:
Track A selected: Drives 53 km north to Kalathagiri Falls. Recommended if driver and rider feel energetic post-lunch.
Time Route Segment / Stop Details, Activities & Equal Group Allocations (5 Pax)[cite: 1]
06:30 AM – 07:15 AM Wake-up, Tea & Day-Bag Prep Generous 45-min group prep window. Pack waterfall dry bags (towels, change of clothes, water shoes).
07:15 AM – 08:15 AM Stay → Mullayanagiri Base (22 km) Misty mountain ascent. Headlights on. Show screenshot of digital entry pass at Kaimara checkpost. Hatchback stays in 1st/2nd gear.
08:15 AM – 10:30 AM Mullayanagiri Peak Trek Activities: Climb the ~450 stone steps to Karnataka's highest peak (1,925 m). Witness panoramic mountain views and rolling clouds.
Buffer: Full 2 hours allocated for an unrushed climb, scenic photos, and safe descent.
10:30 AM – 11:30 AM Attigundi Junction (Breakfast Halt) Roadside Malnad mess: Hot Akki Roti, Guliappa (Paddu) with spicy coconut chutney, and fresh estate filter coffee.
11:45 AM – 02:30 PM Jhari Waterfalls (Dabdabe Falls) Activities & Allocations: Park car and bike safely at entrance barrier. All 5 pax board 1 local 4x4 Jeep (~₹1,000 round-trip) down the steep plantation track.
Driver Relief: The 2.5-hour jeep + waterfall window gives driver and rider a total break from operating vehicles.
Waterfall Bathing Protocol: Change into swimwear/quick-dry clothing; use rubber-grip footwear on slippery boulders; dry off and change back before boarding return jeep.
02:30 PM – 04:00 PM Sit-Down Lunch Halt Leisurely lunch at The Arabica (coastal/Malnad meals) or Dal Roti (pure veg). Extended meal allows solo drivers to fully recharge.
04:00 PM – 06:45 PM Track A: Kalathagiri Falls & Veerabhadra Temple (53 km drive) Scenic 2-lane State Highway through thick tree cover via Santaveri & Lingadahalli. Water plunges 120 meters over boulders directly across the rock-cut entrance of the ancient Veerabhadra Temple. Smooth tarmac, zero off-roading.
07:30 PM onwards Campfire & Estate Dinner Private estate bonfire, music, barbecue/dinner, and a full night's rest after an active outdoor day.

Day 3: Sunday, Oct 4 – Late Checkout, UNESCO Hoysala Heritage & Highway Return

Time Route Segment / Stop Details, Activities & Equal Group Allocations (5 Pax)[cite: 1]
09:00 AM – 10:45 AM Late Breakfast & Leisurely Checkout No Early Alarms: Sleep in, enjoy hot homestyle breakfast on the estate, pack luggage into car boot, tie down bike bags, and check out by 10:45 AM.
10:45 AM – 11:45 AM Stay → Halebidu (38 km) Smooth State Highway passing through rural coconut groves. Easy cruising at 60–75 km/h.
11:45 AM – 01:45 PM Shri Hoysaleswara Temple (Halebidu) UNESCO World Heritage Focus: Twin star-shaped soapstone sanctums, monolithic Nandi pavilions, and unbroken outer friezes carved with intricate relief sculptures.
Group Allocation: Hire 1 authorized ASI guide for the group of 5 (~₹400) to decode carvings.
Pro-Tip: Pack clean, thick cotton socks in your pocket. Footwear is removed at the gate; socks protect feet from hot soapstone floors.
01:45 PM – 02:30 PM Halebidu → Hassan Bypass (30 km) Connect directly onto 4-lane NH 75.
02:30 PM – 03:45 PM Hassan Bypass (Lunch Halt) Relaxed lunch at Hotel Mayura or Empire Restaurant Hassan. Air-conditioned dining, spacious parking, and clean washrooms.
03:45 PM – 06:00 PM Hassan → Yediyur → Kunigal (125 km on NH 75) Straight 4-lane cruising. Maintain convoy speed at 80–85 km/h matching the Meteor 350 comfort sweet spot.
06:00 PM – 06:45 PM Highway Tea Halt at Swathi Delicacy Filter coffee, crispy dosas, and physical stretching for the solo rider before entering city traffic.
06:45 PM – 09:30 PM Kunigal → Nelamangala → NICE Road → Whitefield (85 km) Route via NICE road to bypass downtown traffic. Return home comfortably without exhaustion.

Comprehensive Restaurant Directory (Google 4.5+ • 100+ Reviews)

1. Pure Vegetarian Highlights

  • [Pure Veg] Hotel Pavithra (Kyathsandra / Tumkur) – 4.5+ (14,000+ Reviews). The undisputed king of Thatte Idli. Steaming hot butter idlis, crispy vadas, potato saagu, and filter coffee. Perfect Day 1 breakfast halt.
  • [Pure Veg] Dal Roti (IG Road, Chikmagalur Town) – 4.5+ (1,400+ Reviews). Homestyle North Indian food. Non-greasy phulkas, Dal Tadka, Paneer Butter Masala, and Jeera Rice.
  • [Pure Veg] Swathi Delicacy (NH 75, Near Yediyur) – 4.5+ (32,000+ Reviews). Gold standard highway diner. Ghee Roast Masala Dosa, Chow-Chow Bath, and South Indian filter coffee.
  • [Pure Veg] Sri Mangala Bhavan (Market Road, Chikmagalur) – 4.5+ (850+ Reviews). Traditional Karnataka banana leaf meals, spicy rasam, veg palya, and crisp evening tiffin.
  • [Pure Veg] Paakashala (NH 48, Tumkur Bypass) – 4.5+ (5,200+ Reviews). Excellent sit-down AC highway alternative with pristine washrooms.

2. Multi-Cuisine, Malnad Specialists & Plantation Cafes

  • [Cafe] The Estate Cafe (Mallandur Road) – 4.5+ (800+ Reviews). Open-air wooden deck perched over coffee slopes. Wood-fired thin-crust pizzas, creamy pasta, garlic bread, and cold brews.
  • [Heritage] Town Canteen (Town Center) – 4.5+ (2,500+ Reviews). Chikmagalur's most iconic breakfast institution since 1966. Famous for golden crispy Malnad Benne Dosa, potato sagu, and filter coffee.
  • [Non-Veg] The Arabica Restaurant (Hotel Aadrika) (Milan Theatre Road) – 4.5+ (1,500+ Reviews). Premium sit-down dining. Kundapura Chicken Curry, Anjal Tawa Fry, Neer Dosa, and North Indian vegetarian thalis.
  • [Non-Veg] Gowdru Oota / Malnad Donne Biryani (Town Center) – 4.5+ (650+ Reviews). Authentic mint-coriander Donne Biryani, Chicken Ghee Roast, Mutton Chops, and pepper fry.
  • [Scenic Stop] Siri Coffee Bar (KM Road / Kaimara) – 4.5+ (7,500+ Reviews). Famous roadside plantation cafe featuring the iconic green grass sculpture, fresh estate coffee, and local spices.
