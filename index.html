Attraction Guide & Key Activities

1. Muthanga Wildlife Sanctuary

Sat Morning
Dense jungle corridor bordering Bandipur with heavy elephant concentrations. Entry: ₹110/head | Jeep: ~₹2,200/jeep.
Top Activities: Forest department 4x4 open-jeep safari, elephant herd tracking, nature photography in early morning fog.

2. Edakkal Caves

Sat Mid-Morning
Natural cleft rock formation with 6,000-year-old Neolithic engravings. Entry: ₹50/head | Shuttle: ₹20/head.
Top Activities: Climbing 350+ stone steps, inspecting prehistoric petroglyphs, sweeping views of the three-state border valley.

3. Karapuzha Dam, Adventure Park & Garden

Sat Afternoon
Expansive earthen dam with manicured rose gardens and high-adrenaline rides. Entry: ₹30/head | Zipline: ~₹390.
Top Activities: 600-meter lake zipline, giant swing, human slingshot, stroll through landscaped flower gardens.

4. Kanthanpara Waterfalls

Sat Late Afternoon
Peaceful 30m cascade surrounded by bamboo clusters and rolling tea estates. Entry: ₹50/head.
Top Activities: Easy 150m flat walk, feeling the fresh mountain spray from boardwalks, foot-dipping in clean side-rivulets.

5. En Ooru Tribal Heritage Village

Sun Morning (Optional)
Kerala's first tribal heritage hamlet set along a scenic ridge. Entry: ₹50/head | Shuttle: ₹30/head.
Top Activities: Visiting traditional thatched huts, bamboo bow-and-arrow archery, sampling wild herbal tea (Kattappi).

6. Pookode Lake & Lakkidi View Point

Sun Mid-Day
Natural freshwater mountain lake + panoramic mountain pass vista. Entry: ₹40/head | Lakkidi: Free.
Top Activities: Shaded walking loop around the lake, viewing water lilies, gazing down the 9 hairpin turns of Thamarassery Ghat.

7. Karlad Lake & Banasura Sagar Dam

Sun Afternoon
Karlad adventure sports + Asia's 2nd largest earthen dam. Karlad Zipline: ~₹300 | Dam Entry: ₹40 | Speedboat: ~₹1,000/boat.
Top Activities: High-speed zipline across Karlad, 5-passenger speed boating cutting across Banasura reservoir islands, sunset dam walk.
