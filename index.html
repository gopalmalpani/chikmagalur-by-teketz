Things to Do & Try in Kerala (For First-Time Visitors)

  • Drink "Kattappi" (Tribal Herbal Black Tea): Brewed at En Ooru using dry ginger (chukku), cardamom, black pepper, and unrefined jaggery.
  • Eat Hot Coconut-Oil Fried Banana Chips (*Nendran Upperi*): Pick up freshly fried batches sliced right before your eyes from the hot frying vats in Sulthan Bathery or Kalpetta market.
  • Pair Kerala Malabar Parotta with Roast Gravy: Multi-layered, flaky, buttery flatbread that pairs perfectly with spicy chicken, beef, or vegetable roast.
  • Experience Authentic Banana-Leaf *Sadhya*: Try a sit-down vegetarian banquet featuring red matta rice, avial (vegetables in coconut/curd), sambar, thoran, pulissery, and sweet ada pradhaman (payasam).
  • Drink Spiced Passion Fruit Juice: Street vendors along the pathway of Edakkal Caves blend fresh local passion fruit with mint and bird's eye chili (*kanthari*).
  • Shop for Single-Origin Wayanadan Spices: Look for fresh green cardamom, whole black tellicherry peppercorns, cloves, and wild forest honey directly from tribal outlets or spice cooperatives.

Essential Trip Tips & Things to Remember

  • No Single-Use Plastic: Wayanad has a strict zero-plastic policy at eco-tourism points (Kanthanpara, Karlad, Edakkal, Banasura). Forest checkpoints inspect bags. If carrying packaged mineral water bottles, you must pay a ₹10–₹20 refundable deposit sticker fee at the gate.
  • Carry Cash for Spot Bookings: The ticket counters at Muthanga Safari, Edakkal shuttles, and parking lots have weak cellular network reception. UPI often fails—keep a cash pool of around ₹5,000–₹6,000 ready with one coordinator.
  • Grippy Footwear is Essential: In September, the 350+ stone steps of Edakkal Caves and the walkways near Kanthanpara Falls have wet moss and can be slippery. Avoid smooth flat soles or heels.
  • Pre-order Group Meals: For a group of 12, sitting down and ordering a la carte will cause 45-minute delays. Have one person call the restaurant 20 minutes before arrival to pre-order 12 meals/thalis.
  • Banasura Dam Speed Boat Coordination: Speed boats seat only 5 passengers each. The 12-person group must divide into 3 boats (e.g., 5 + 5 + 2). Head straight to the boating counter upon arrival, as ticket issuance stops around 5:00 PM – 5:15 PM.
  • Strict Forest Highway Etiquette in Bandipur: Driving through Bandipur between 6:00 AM and 6:45 AM requires zero honking, no stopping the vehicle on the roadway, and absolutely no stepping out for animal selfies. Fines are strictly enforced by forest mobile patrols.
