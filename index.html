Bangalore Bus Packing Checklist (12-Seater Tempo Traveller)

  • Water & Hydration: Two 20-litre bubble tops with a manual dispenser pump, or two cartons of 1-litre sealed bottles. Refill personal bottles to comply with Wayanad's strict anti-plastic checkpoints.
  • Midnight Highway Munchies: Theplas, dry khakhras, roasted makhanas, bhujia, NutriChoice/Oreo biscuits, protein bars, and cup noodles for villa late nights.
  • Electrolytes & Digestion: ORS/Tang sachets for hydration after climbing 350+ stone steps at Edakkal; Pudin Hara, Digene, Eno, and motion sickness tablets (Avomine / Ondansetron) for winding ghat curves.
  • First-Aid & Relief: Moov / Volini spray for tired calves, Paracetamol (Dolo 650), Band-Aids, Dettol antiseptic wipes, and Odomos mosquito repellent.
  • Group Electronics: 2 multi-plug spike surge extension boards (for villa bed charging), auxiliary audio cable / Bluetooth FM transmitter for the bus, and phone car chargers.
  • Shisha Kit (Strict Protocol): Hookah base, hoses, coconut charcoal cubes, portable electric coil burner, wind cover, foil, and flavors packed deep in the rear boot luggage. Never light or open near Bandipur or Muthanga forest checkpoints.
  • Luggage Rule: Strictly one soft backpack or medium duffel bag per person. Avoid large rigid hard-shell suitcases to prevent clogging the TT boot.

Essential Trip Tips & Logistics

  • Strict Zero-Plastic Policy: Wayanad strictly bans single-use plastic bottles at eco-tourism points (Kanthanpara, Karlad, Edakkal, Banasura). Forest checkpoints charge a refundable ₹10–₹20 sticker deposit on mineral water bottles.
  • Carry Pooled Cash: Mobile data is patchy around Muthanga Safari, Edakkal shuttles, and parking lots. UPI often fails—keep a cash pool of around ₹6,000 with one designated treasurer.
  • Grippy Footwear: In September, the 350+ stone steps of Edakkal Caves and walkways near Kanthanpara Falls have wet moss and can be slippery. Wear shoes with firm rubber traction.
  • Pre-order Group Meals: For 12 people, ordering a la carte causes 45-minute delays. Have one person call the restaurant 20 minutes ahead to pre-order 12 vegetarian thalis.
  • Bandipur Forest Driving Rules: Zero honking, no stopping on the roadway, and no stepping out for animal selfies. Fines are strictly enforced by forest patrols.
