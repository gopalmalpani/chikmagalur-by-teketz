Wayanad Weekend Blueprint (12 Pax Group)

Dates: 11th Sept Midnight – 14th Sept Early Morning | Vehicle: 12-Seater Tempo Traveller | Stay Hub: Kalpetta Town / Chundale

Curfew Warning & Exit Strategy: Bandipur Highway closes from 9:00 PM to 6:00 AM. On Sunday night, we exit northward via Kattikulam → Kutta → Gonikoppa → Hunsur (Zero night closure; completely open 24/7).

Detailed Time-Wise Route: Option A (High-Trekker / Soochipara Falls)

Features the massive 200m roaring falls. Involves steep 1.5 km downhill and uphill hike. Hotel check-in is at 5:45 PM.

Day & Time Route Segment / Point-to-Point Transit Activity, Food & Notes
Fri 11:30 PM – 03:00 AM Bangalore → Mandya → Mysore Ring Road Smooth cruise via Mysore-Bangalore Expressway.
Sat 03:00 AM – 05:15 AM Mysore → Nanjangud → Gundlupet Drive towards border. Short restroom break before the forest gate.
Sat 05:15 AM – 06:00 AM Gundlupet Checkpost Gate Wait in line for Bandipur forest checkpost gate opening (6:00 AM sharp).
Sat 06:00 AM – 06:45 AM Gundlupet → Bandipur → Muthanga Gate Early morning wildlife corridor transit into Kerala.
Sat 06:45 AM – 08:15 AM Muthanga Wildlife Sanctuary Stop 1: Morning open jeep safari (~₹5,000 for two jeeps). Spot wild elephants, deer, and gaur.
Sat 08:15 AM – 08:30 AM Muthanga → Sulthan Bathery (12 km) Transit to breakfast.
Sat 08:30 AM – 09:30 AM Sulthan Bathery Town Breakfast Halt: Veg: Saravana Bhavan / Mint Flower | Non-Veg: Wilton Restaurant.
Sat 09:30 AM – 10:00 AM Sulthan Bathery → Edakkal Caves (14 km) Scenic drive through Ambalavayal.
Sat 10:00 AM – 12:30 PM Edakkal Caves (Ambukuthi Mala) Stop 2: Shuttle jeep to base + climb 350+ stone steps (~₹70/head). 6,000-yr petroglyphs.
Sat 12:30 PM – 01:00 PM Edakkal Caves → Meppadi (18 km) Transit through tea country.
Sat 01:00 PM – 02:00 PM Meppadi Town Lunch Halt: Veg: Hotel Sanman Veg | Non-Veg: Chembra Heritage.
Sat 02:00 PM – 02:30 PM Meppadi → Soochipara Falls Parking (12 km) Descent toward Sentinel Rock estate roads.
Sat 02:30 PM – 05:00 PM Soochipara Waterfalls (Sentinel Rock) Stop 3: 1.5 km steep hike down through tea estates to the roaring 200m falls (~₹80/head). Hike back up.
Sat 05:00 PM – 05:45 PM Soochipara → Kalpetta (24 km) Drive to central town accommodation.
Sat 05:45 PM – 08:00 PM Kalpetta (Stay Visit 1) Check-in & Rest: Hot showers, unpack, and unwind after the demanding trek.
Sat 08:00 PM – 08:15 PM Stay → Kalpetta Town Center Short drive/walk to dinner.
Sat 08:15 PM – 09:45 PM Kalpetta Town Dinner: Veg: Hotel Woodlands Pure Veg | Non-Veg: 1980's A Nostalgic Restaurant.
Sat 09:45 PM onwards Kalpetta (Stay Visit 2) Overnight Stay: Sleep and recharge.
Sun 07:30 AM – 08:30 AM Kalpetta Stay Checkout, load luggage into Traveller, and breakfast.
Sun 08:30 AM – 09:15 AM Kalpetta → Vythiri → En Ooru (16 km) Morning transit to south hills.
Sun 09:15 AM – 10:45 AM En Ooru Tribal Village Stop 4: Shuttle jeep to thatched huts, tribal handicrafts, tea stall, and ridge viewpoints (~₹80/head).
Sun 10:45 AM – 11:00 AM En Ooru → Pookode Lake (4 km) Short drive down the hill.
Sun 11:00 AM – 11:45 AM Pookode Lake & Lakkidi View Point Stop 5: Peaceful forest lake walk + 10-minute roadside stop at Lakkidi for ghat mist and valley views.
Sun 11:45 AM – 12:15 PM Lakkidi → Vythiri / Chundale Drive towards lunch junction.
Sun 12:15 PM – 01:15 PM Vythiri / Chundale Bypass Lunch Halt: Veg: Hotel Haritagiri Pure Veg | Non-Veg: Wayanad Treat.
Sun 01:15 PM – 01:45 PM Chundale → Karlad Lake (16 km) Drive through scenic Pozhuthana tea estates.
Sun 01:45 PM – 03:00 PM Karlad Lake (Adventure Stop) Stop 6: High-speed zipline across the water (~₹300) and kayaking.
Sun 03:00 PM – 03:30 PM Karlad Lake → Banasura Sagar Dam (8 km) Direct connection to Padinjarathara.
Sun 03:30 PM – 06:15 PM Banasura Sagar Dam Stop 7: Shuttle buggy to crest, speed boating across islands (~₹250/head), and sunset walk.
Sun 06:15 PM – 07:15 PM Padinjarathara → Mananthavady (24 km) Direct northward drive (bypasses Kalpetta/Sulthan Bathery completely).
Sun 07:15 PM – 08:00 PM Mananthavady → Kattikulam → Kutta Cross Kerala border into Karnataka (Zero curfew route).
Sun 08:00 PM – 08:45 PM Kutta → Ponnampet → Gonikoppa (30 km) Scenic South Coorg highway.
Sun 08:45 PM – 09:45 PM Gonikoppa → Anechoukur → Hunsur Bypass Passing 24/7 open forest checkpost into Hunsur.
Sun 09:45 PM – 10:45 PM Hunsur Highway Bypass Dinner Halt: Veg: Aadishakti Pure Veg / Mayura Veg | Non-Veg: Highway Delight Dhaba.
Sun 10:45 PM – 03:00 AM Hunsur → Mysore → Bangalore Cruise down Mysore-Bangalore Expressway back to Bangalore drop-off.
