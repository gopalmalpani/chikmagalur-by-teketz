MANDATORY PROTOCOLS • 5-Pax Group & Convoy Safeguards:
• Strict Dry Day (Oct 2): Gandhi Jayanti is a 100% legal dry day across Karnataka. Purchase all homestay alcohol in Bangalore on Thursday, Oct 1st and transport sealed in car boot.
• Mullayanagiri Vehicle Entry Pass: Pre-book Slot 1 (6:00 AM – 12:00 PM) on the official portal (pgbiz.omniware.in/chikkamagalurutourism). Download or screenshot QR pass prior to ascent (cellular data drops at Kaimara checkpost).
• Solo Driver & Rider Relief: Exactly 1 car driver and 1 biker. Highway speed is capped at 75–85 km/h. If driver/rider fatigue hits post-Jhari Falls on Day 2, toggle to Track B (Hidden Gems) to skip 106 km of driving.
• Boot Space Rule: 5 adults in a hatchback requires soft duffel bags or backpacks only. Strictly zero hard-shell suitcases.
• Mandatory Mountain Fuel Top-up: Top up to 100% full tank in Chikmagalur town on Friday evening. Zero pumps exist in the Bababudangiri hill range!

Day 2: Saturday, Oct 3 – Mullayanagiri Peak, Jhari Waterfall Dip & Afternoon Circuit

Driver Fatigue Management • Afternoon Plan Selector:
Track A selected: Drives 53 km north to Kalathagiri Falls. Recommended if driver and rider feel energetic post-lunch.
Time Route Segment / Stop Details, Activities & Equal Group Allocations (5 Pax)[cite: 1]
06:30 AM – 07:15 AM Wake-up, Tea & Day-Bag Prep Generous 45-min group prep window. Pack waterfall dry bags (towels, change of clothes, water shoes). Check fuel gauges (tanks were filled yesterday).
07:15 AM – 08:15 AM Stay → Mullayanagiri Base (22 km)
📍 Base GPS
Misty mountain ascent. Headlights on. Show screenshot of digital entry pass at Kaimara checkpost. Hatchback stays in 1st/2nd gear.
08:15 AM – 10:30 AM Mullayanagiri Peak Trek
📍 Summit Trek GPS
Activities: Climb the ~450 stone steps to Karnataka's highest peak (1,925 m). Witness panoramic mountain views and rolling clouds.
Buffer: Full 2 hours allocated for an unrushed climb, scenic photos, and safe descent.
10:30 AM – 11:30 AM Attigundi Junction (Breakfast Halt) Roadside Malnad mess: Hot Akki Roti, Guliappa (Paddu) with spicy coconut chutney, and fresh estate filter coffee.
11:45 AM – 02:30 PM Jhari Waterfalls (Dabdabe Falls)
📍 Jhari GPS
Activities & Allocations: Park car and bike safely at entrance barrier. All 5 pax board 1 local 4x4 Jeep (~₹1,000 round-trip) down the steep plantation track.
Driver Relief: The 2.5-hour jeep + waterfall window gives driver and rider a total break from operating vehicles.
Waterfall Bathing Protocol: Change into swimwear/quick-dry clothing; use rubber-grip footwear on slippery boulders; dry off and change back before boarding return jeep.
02:30 PM – 04:00 PM Sit-Down Lunch Halt (Kailash Parbat Pure Veg / The Arabica)
📍 Kailash Parbat GPS
Leisurely lunch at Kailash Parbat (pure veg North/South Indian) or The Arabica (coastal/Malnad meals). Extended meal allows solo drivers to fully recharge.
04:00 PM – 06:45 PM Track A: Kalathagiri Falls & Veerabhadra Temple (53 km drive)
📍 Kalathagiri Falls GPS 📍 Veerabhadra Temple GPS
📍 Kavikalmatta GPS 📍 Honnammana Halla GPS 📍 Estate Cafe GPS
Scenic 2-lane State Highway through thick tree cover via Santaveri & Lingadahalli. Water plunges 120 meters over boulders directly across the rock-cut entrance of the ancient Veerabhadra Temple. Smooth tarmac, zero off-roading.
07:30 PM onwards Campfire & Estate Dinner Private estate bonfire, music, barbecue/dinner, and a full night's rest after an active outdoor day.

Day 3: Sunday, Oct 4 – Late Checkout, UNESCO Heritage & Bangalore Return

Bangalore Return Route Options (Choose What Fits the Group Vibe):
Option 1 Selected: 275 km direct highway cruise. Enjoy highway dinner at Swathi Delicacy (Yediyur), then enter Bangalore smoothly via NICE Road. Reach Whitefield by 08:45 PM – 09:15 PM!
Time Route Segment / Stop Details, Activities & Convoy Schedule (5 Pax)[cite: 1]
09:00 AM – 10:45 AM Late Breakfast & Leisurely Checkout No Early Alarms: Sleep in, enjoy hot homestyle breakfast on the estate, pack luggage into car boot, tie down bike bags, and check out by 10:45 AM.
10:45 AM – 11:45 AM Stay → Halebidu (38 km) Smooth State Highway passing through rural coconut groves. Easy cruising at 60–75 km/h.
11:45 AM – 01:45 PM Shri Hoysaleswara Temple (Halebidu)
📍 Halebidu GPS
UNESCO World Heritage Focus: Twin star-shaped soapstone sanctums, monolithic Nandi pavilions, and unbroken outer friezes carved with intricate relief sculptures.
Group Allocation: Hire 1 authorized ASI guide for the group of 5 (~₹400) to decode carvings.
Pro-Tip: Pack clean, thick cotton socks in your pocket. Footwear is removed at the gate; socks protect feet from hot soapstone floors.
01:45 PM – 02:30 PM Halebidu → Hassan Bypass (30 km) Connect directly onto 4-lane NH 75.
02:30 PM – 03:45 PM Hassan Bypass (Lunch Halt & Top-Up) Relaxed lunch at Hotel Mayura or Empire Restaurant Hassan. Air-conditioned dining, spacious parking, and clean washrooms.
⛽ REFILL STOP 2 BPCL COCO Hassan Bypass (Top up both vehicles for the 180 km return run).
03:45 PM – 06:15 PM Hassan → Yediyur → Kunigal (125 km on NH 75) Straight 4-lane cruising. Maintain convoy speed at 80–85 km/h matching the Meteor 350 comfort sweet spot.
06:15 PM – 07:15 PM Swathi Delicacy (NH 75, Yediyur) • Highway Dinner / Snacks Filter coffee, crispy ghee roast dosas, and physical stretching. If taking the Direct Express route, have early dinner here to enter Bangalore completely fed with zero detour!
07:15 PM – 09:15 PM Kunigal → Nelamangala → NICE Road → Whitefield (85 km) Connect directly from NH 75 onto NICE Road at Nelamangala toll plaza. Bypass city traffic bottlenecks. Reach home by ~09:15 PM!
